# Project Brief 

## Status 
Completed 

## Project 
Pass-A-Grille Fishing Charters UX Research & Redesign Case Study 

## Overview 
This project explores why individuals interested in booking fishing charter experiences do not consistently progress from initial interest to confirmed reservations.

## Problem Statement 
Individuals interested in booking fishing charter experiences are not consistently progressing from initial interest to confirmed reservations, indicating breakdowns in how services are discovered, evaluated and trusted during the decision-making process. 

## Goal
To understand user motivations, decision-making behaviors, trust signals, and booking barriers in order to inform a more effective user-centered digital experience.
