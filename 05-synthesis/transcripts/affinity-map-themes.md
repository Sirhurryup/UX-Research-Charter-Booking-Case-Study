# Affinity Map Themes

## Theme 1: Clarity Builds Confidence
Users need clear information about what is included, trip duration, what to bring, and how packages differ before they feel comfortable booking.

## Theme 2: Confidence Builders
Reviews, testimonials, captain expertise, and personal contact help users feel reassured and confident.

## Theme 3: Value Extends Beyond Price
Users evaluate value through amenities, service quality, atmosphere, and expected outcomes—not price alone.

## Theme 4: The Experience Is Social and Emotional
Fishing charters are opportunities for family bonding, relaxation, adventure, and memory-making.

## Theme 5: Flexibility Supports Conversion
Users want the convenience of online booking combined with the ability to ask questions by phone.
