# Behavioral Personas

## 👤 Persona 1: The Memory-Seeking Planner

### Description

A user who wants to create meaningful experiences with family and friends and carefully evaluates options to ensure the outing will be enjoyable and worthwhile.

### Characteristics
- Plans around family visits or special occasions
- Values relaxation and connection
- Compares price, reviews, and amenities
- Wants the experience to feel memorable

### Goals
- Create lasting memories
- Enjoy quality time with others
- Feel confident the trip will be worth the cost

### Pain Points
- Unclear package differences
- Limited detail about what is included
- Uncertainty about the overall experience

### Needs
- Clear descriptions
- Transparent pricing
- Reviews and testimonials
- Family-oriented messaging

## Persona 2: The Confidence-Seeking First-Timer

### Description
A user who is interested in trying a fishing charter for the first time but wants enough information to feel confident that the trip will be enjoyable and worth the investment.

### Characteristics
- Has never booked a fishing charter before
- Comfortable booking online, but may want to call with questions first
- Reads reviews and testimonials before making a decision
- Pays close attention to what is included and what to expect

### Goals
- Understand exactly what they are paying for
- Feel confident that the captain will provide a good experience
- Avoid wasting money on a disappointing trip
- Book with minimal stress or uncertainty

### Pain Points
- Unclear descriptions of what is included
- Difficulty understanding how packages differ
- Limited information about the captain and crew
- Concern about booking an experience that does not meet expectations

### Needs
- Detailed package descriptions
- Information about the captain’s experience and personality
- Reviews from previous customers
- Clear ways to call, text, or book online without hassle
- Straightforward explanations of what to bring and what is provided
