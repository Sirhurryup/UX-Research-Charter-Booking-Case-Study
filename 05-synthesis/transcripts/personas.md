# Behavioral Personas

## 👤 Persona 1: The Memory-Seeking Planner

## Description

A user who wants to create meaningful experiences with family and friends and carefully evaluates options to ensure the outing will be enjoyable and worthwhile.

## Characteristics
Plans around family visits or special occasions
Values relaxation and connection
Compares price, reviews, and amenities
Wants the experience to feel memorable

## Goals
Create lasting memories
Enjoy quality time with others
Feel confident the trip will be worth the cost

## Pain Points
Unclear package differences
Limited detail about what is included
Uncertainty about the overall experience

## Needs
Clear descriptions
Transparent pricing
Reviews and testimonials
Family-oriented messaging

---

## 👤 Persona 2: The Confidence-Seeking First-Timer

## Description

A user who is interested in trying a charter but needs reassurance and detailed information before feeling comfortable booking.

## Characteristics
May never have booked a charter before
Prefers to call with questions
Relies heavily on reviews and social proof
Needs to understand what to expect

## Goals
Feel reassured before committing
Understand what is included
Avoid making a poor decision

## Pain Points
Lack of trust signals
Incomplete information
Uncertainty about what will happen

## Needs
Captain introduction
Frequently asked questions
Clear package comparisons
Prominent contact options
