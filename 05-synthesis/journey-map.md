# Current-State Journey Map

## Overview

This journey map illustrates the steps users take when considering, evaluating, booking, and experiencing a fishing charter. It highlights motivations, questions, emotions, and pain points across the process.

---

## Stage 1: Awareness

### User Goal
Discover an enjoyable outdoor experience for family, friends, or personal relaxation.

### User Questions
- What can we do together?
- Is this something we would enjoy?
- Is this worth considering?

### Emotions
Curious, excited, hopeful

### Pain Points
- Unclear why this experience is different from other activities
- Weak messaging that does not capture the emotional value

---

## Stage 2: Research and Comparison

### User Goal
Compare charter options and determine which experience best fits their needs.

### User Questions
- What is included?
- How much does it cost?
- What makes this charter unique?
- Can I trust the captain and crew?

### Emotions
Interested, analytical, cautious

### Pain Points
- Limited package details
- Weak trust signals
- Difficulty comparing offerings

---

## Stage 3: Booking Decision

### User Goal
Feel confident enough to reserve the charter.

### User Questions
- Am I making the right decision?
- Can I contact someone if I have questions?
- Is this worth the investment?

### Emotions
Excited, uncertain, hesitant

### Pain Points
- Incomplete information
- Lack of reassurance
- Limited booking flexibility

---

## Stage 4: Preparation

### User Goal
Understand what to bring and what to expect.

### User Questions
- What should I bring?
- Where do we meet?
- What happens if the weather changes?

### Emotions
Prepared, slightly anxious

### Pain Points
- Missing logistical details
- Unclear expectations

---

## Stage 5: On-the-Water Experience

### User Goal
Enjoy a memorable, relaxing, and rewarding outing.

### User Questions
- Will we have a good time?
- Will the crew be helpful?
- Will this feel worth it?

### Emotions
Excited, engaged, connected

### Pain Points
- Service quality directly shapes satisfaction

---

## Stage 6: Reflection and Repeat Booking

### User Goal
Decide whether the experience was worth repeating.

### User Questions
- Would I do this again?
- Would I recommend it to others?

### Emotions
Satisfied, delighted, disappointed

### Pain Points
- Negative experiences reduce repeat booking and referrals
