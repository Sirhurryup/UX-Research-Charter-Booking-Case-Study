## Core Interview Questions (Value * Experience Understanding) 

1. Can you walk me through the last time you considered or booked a fishing charter? 

2. What stood out to you when deciding whether a charter was worth booking? 

3. What information helped you understand what the experience would be like? 

4. Was there anything that made it difficult to understand what you would get from the experience? 

5. What makes a fishing charter feel like a "great experience" to you? 

6. After a charter, what would make you want to book again?
