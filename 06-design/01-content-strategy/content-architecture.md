# Content Architecture

## Purpose

This content architecture maps research findings to the user decision-making process.

The goal is to ensure users receive the right information at the right time as they move from initial interest to booking confirmation.

---

# User Decision Journey

## Stage 1: Trust

Primary Question:

"Can I trust this charter?"

### Homepage

Content:

- Captain Credibility
- Safety Information
- Customer Testimonials
- Fishing Reports
- Trip Packages

Outcome:

Users gain confidence that the charter is reputable, experienced, and capable of delivering a safe and enjoyable experience.

---

## Stage 2: Evaluation

Primary Question:

"Is this trip right for me?"

### Trip Details Page

Content:

- Trip Packages
- Captain Information
- Fishing Reports
- Launch Information
- Customer Testimonials
- Safety Information

Outcome:

Users compare options and determine whether the experience matches their goals, budget, and expectations.

---

## Stage 3: Commitment

Primary Question:

"Am I ready to reserve?"

### Booking Flow

Content:

- Package Selection
- Guest Information
- Cancellation Policy
- Launch Information
- Booking Confirmation

Outcome:

Users complete the reservation with confidence and clarity.
