# Site Content Prioritization

## Purpose

Content was prioritized based on its ability to reduce uncertainty during the booking decision process.

---

## Must Have

- Trip Packages
- Booking Experience
- Captain Credibility
- Safety Information

### Why

These directly answer:

- What am I buying?
- Who am I trusting?
- Am I safe?
- How do I reserve?

Removing these sections would make booking significantly more difficult.

---

## Should Have

- Customer Testimonials
- Launch Information
- Fishing Reports

### Why

These strengthen confidence and reduce uncertainty.

- Testimonials provide social proof.
- Launch information reduces day-of-trip confusion.
- Fishing reports demonstrate activity and expertise.

---

## Nice To Have

Future enhancements that improve the experience but are not required to make a booking decision.
