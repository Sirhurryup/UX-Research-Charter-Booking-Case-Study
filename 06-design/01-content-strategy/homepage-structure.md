# Homepage Structure

## Purpose

The homepage is designed to guide users through the booking decision process by progressively answering key questions identified during user research.

The structure follows a Trust → Evaluation → Commitment framework.

---

## 1. Hero Section

Primary Question:

"What is this experience?"

Purpose:

Introduce the charter experience and encourage users to explore available trips.

Content:

- Headline
- Supporting text
- Hero image
- Primary CTA

---

## 2. Trip Packages

Primary Question:

"What am I buying?"

Purpose:

Help users understand available trip options.

Content:

- Package types
- Duration
- Pricing
- Included features

---

## 3. Meet The Captain

Primary Question:

"Who am I trusting?"

Purpose:

Build credibility and confidence.

Content:

- Captain photo
- Captain story
- Experience
- Certifications
- Fishing specialties

---

## 4. Customer Testimonials

Primary Question:

"Did people like me enjoy this experience?"

Purpose:

Reduce uncertainty through social proof.

Content:

- Customer photo
- Customer name
- Review quote
- Trip type
- Rating

---

## 5. Safety Information

Primary Question:

"Will I be safe?"

Purpose:

Address risk and emergency concerns.

Content:

- Safety certifications
- CPR / First Aid training
- Emergency procedures
- Weather policy
- Refund policy

---

## 6. Fishing Reports

Primary Question:

"Can this charter consistently produce results?"

Purpose:

Demonstrate recent activity and expertise.

Content:

- Recent catches
- Date-stamped reports
- Species in season
- Weather conditions
- Success stories

---

## 7. Booking CTA

Primary Question:

"Am I ready to reserve?"

Purpose:

Provide a clear path to booking.

Content:

- CTA button
- Contact information
- Availability
