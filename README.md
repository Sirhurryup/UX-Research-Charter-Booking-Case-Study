# Pass-A-Grille Fishing Charters UX Research & Design Case Study

## Project Summary 
This project explores why individuals interested in booking fishing charter experiences do not consistently progress from initial interest to confirmed reservations. The research focuse on how users discover, evaluate, and trust charter services during the decision-making process.

## Problem Statement
Individuals interested in booking fishing charter experiences are not consistently progressing from initial interest to confirmed reservations, indicating breakdowns in how services are discovered, evaluated, and trusted during the decision-making process.
